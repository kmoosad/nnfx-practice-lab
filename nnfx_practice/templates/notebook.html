How to read the trades table
Trade sets
Trade 1 and Trade 2 belong to the same entry and share a trade set ID. Newest sets appear first. Filters may show only one trade from a set.
Reporting period
Practice trading uses one starting balance for the notebook. Older records before that starting point remain visible.
Units: entry / current
Position size when the trade opened and the units still open at the last recorded update. Positive units indicate a long trade; negative units indicate a short trade. Planned units show the intended size.
Profit / loss
Final pips and profit or loss are shown only after a trade fully closes. Partial closes can already affect account totals. Financing, commission and other charges are shown separately.
Move stop to entry
Applies to Trade 2 after Trade 1 closes at its take-profit price. A requested stop change is shown as confirmed only when the broker confirms it.
Set entry status
Complete means both trades and their required protection were confirmed. This describes how the set was opened, even after a trade closes.
Missing values
Not recorded means no value is available in this notebook; it does not mean zero. Trades that are still open have no final exit yet.
